ConvoPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Information We Process

ConvoPilot processes Facebook Page messages and sender identifiers received through Meta's Messenger Platform to provide automated customer-support replies. Depending on configuration, message records may be stored in the application's database.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to respond to customer messages, maintain conversation history where enabled, and operate and troubleshoot the service. Information is not intentionally sold.

Data Sharing and Retention

Messages may be processed by the configured AI provider and infrastructure providers when required to operate the service. Retention depends on the application's configuration and operational requirements.

Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information processed by ConvoPilot, contact the application operator using the contact details provided in the app's official contact information. Include enough information to identify the relevant conversation without sending passwords or access tokens.

Contact

Contact the ConvoPilot application operator through the official contact channel provided for this app.
